Scroll-Triggered Animations

A comprehensive guide to creating engaging scroll-based effects

Fade-In Animation

The most subtle scroll animation. Elements smoothly transition from invisible to visible as they enter the viewport. Perfect for text content and soft reveals.

🎭

Slide-Up Animation

Elements rise from below their final position. This creates a sense of content emerging from beneath the page, commonly used for cards and content blocks.

Horizontal Slide Animations

Elements can slide in from the left or right, creating directional flow and guiding the user's eye across the page.

←

Slide From Left

Slide From Right

Scale-Up Animation

Elements start smaller and grow to their full size. This creates a sense of importance and draws attention to featured content.

Featured Content

The scale animation makes this box feel like it's coming toward you, creating depth and emphasizing its importance in the page hierarchy.

Rotate-In Animation

Combines rotation with scale for a dynamic, playful entrance. Best used sparingly for decorative elements or to add personality to the design.

⭐

Blur-In Animation

Elements start out of focus and gradually sharpen, creating a cinematic effect similar to a camera focusing on a subject.

📸

The Focus Effect

This animation technique mimics the experience of viewing something come into focus, making it particularly effective for photography portfolios, image galleries, and hero sections.
