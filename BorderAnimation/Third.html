Magnetic Pull

Hover · SaaS

Border segments are scattered at rest, then magnetically snap together on hover using CSS transitions with elastic easing. Seen on Vercel AI SDK docs and Clerk auth pages.

Radar Ping

Monitoring · DevOps

Two out-of-phase concentric border rings scale up and fade out, simulating a live radar or sonar ping. Dominant in Datadog alerts, PagerDuty status dashboards, and incident tools.

03

Fire Border

AI · Generative UI

A blurred orange-amber gradient flickers from the bottom edge like real fire using subtle scale animation with alternate direction. Seen on Notion AI processing states and ChatGPT thinking indicators.

DNA Helix Orbit

Biotech · Health SaaS

Two glowing dots travel the perimeter in exact opposite directions, creating a chasing / double-helix effect. Popular in biotech SaaS and health-tech dashboard UIs.

Frost Glass Shine

Glassmorphism · Apple

A frosted glass card with a crisp white top edge highlight that sweeps across slowly — replicating real light on etched glass. Seen in Apple Vision Pro UI concepts and Spline 3D renders.

Rainbow Snake

Motion · Generative

A short rainbow-gradient stroke races endlessly around the border perimeter using SVG stroke-dashoffset — smoother than CSS-only approaches. Featured in Framer Motion demos and Pika.art's generator UI.

Terminal · Retro Dev

A glowing horizontal scan line travels top-to-bottom over a subtle CRT raster pattern, evoking old-school terminal aesthetics. The defining style of Warp, Fig, and Charm.sh CLI tools.

Prism Dispersion

/@property/ · Premium

A full-spectrum conic gradient rotates around the border using CSS @property, simulating light through a prism. Seen in Apple Intelligence UI leaks, iOS 18 concepts, and luxury landing pages.

Stacked Offset Rings

Depth · Design Systems

Three concentric borders at different opacities and offsets gently breathe in opposite directions, creating a layered depth illusion. Popularized by Reshaped UI, shadcn/ui theme demos, and Radix component showcases.

Editorial · Portfolio
